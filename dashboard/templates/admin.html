Halt trading
Stops new trades. Open positions keep their stops. Engine stays up.

   
Resume
Clears the halt and leaves standby.

   
Shutdown (standby)
Halts trading and idles the engine. The process stays alive (a supervisor would restart a real exit). Use Resume to wake it.

Engine exits cleanly; the container/systemd supervisor starts it again.
